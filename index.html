Zander W. Blasingame

Postdoctoral Fellow · Aithyra · Vienna

I am a postdoctoral fellow at Aithyra in Vienna, working with Alex Tong on generative modelling, stochatic processes, and sampling to solve fundamental problems in natural sciences, e.g., sampling from Boltzmann distributions. Aithyra is a new research institute led by Michael Bronstein working at the interesection of machine learning and life sciences.

Previously, I received my Ph.D. in Electrical and Computer Engineering from Clarkson University in 2025, supervised by Chen Liu, focused on controllable generation with flow/diffusion models and downstream applications in biometrics.
